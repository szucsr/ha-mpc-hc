# AI Policy

We welcome the use of AI tools, including large language models, to assist with contributions to this project. That said, full accountability rests with you for anything you submit, just as we are accountable for everything we merge and release. Every contribution is held to a strict quality standard.

Our maintainers invest valuable time and expertise into evaluating submissions. Sending in AI-generated material that you haven't personally verified and comprehended disrespects their effort and will result in rejection.

## Autonomous agents

**Contributions generated or submitted by autonomous agents are strictly prohibited.** Any pull request or issue suspected of being created without direct human control will be closed, and automated replies may be flagged as spam—especially when they ignore our standard issue or PR templates.

## Communication on issues, pull requests, and code reviews

Using AI to refine your writing is acceptable, provided you never let tools publish unverified text under your name. Keep your messages concise and focused on your core point; comments that look like raw, unedited AI output may be hidden.

When submitting a pull request, you must be capable of explaining your changes independently, both in the PR summary and when answering follow-up questions. If an AI drafts your summary, double-check that every technical detail is accurate.

**Never rely on AI to draft answers to maintainer inquiries.** You need a solid grasp of your own work to discuss it properly. Polishing spelling or phrasing with AI is permitted, provided the underlying ideas and technical details come directly from you.

When referencing output from an AI session in your comments, place it inside a blockquote (`>`) and clearly label its source. Always include your own explanation detailing why it matters and what it implies, and avoid pasting lengthy excerpts.

## Non-native English speakers

We understand that AI can bridge language gaps for contributors who do not speak English natively. Refining the grammar or readability of text you authored is completely fine. When translating your thoughts with AI, make sure the output faithfully captures what you meant to say. Placing your original text inside a collapsible `<details>` section demonstrates genuine effort, allows maintainers to cross-check the translation if necessary, and keeps threads easy to follow.

## Code and documentation contributions

While AI can speed up drafting code and documentation, the open-source foundation of our work demands active human oversight from someone who genuinely grasps the AI's output.

You must thoroughly inspect and comprehend all code or text before submitting it, ensuring you can justify every line in your pull request. Submissions that appear to be unvetted AI output will be closed immediately without review.

## Enforcement

Contributions that violate these rules will be closed, and persistent non-compliance can lead to a ban from further participation. If you think your work was closed by mistake, feel free to contact a maintainer to talk it through.
